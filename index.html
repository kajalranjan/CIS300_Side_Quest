About Me

Hi, I’m K, the creator of SideQuest.

This website helps you capture all the activities and adventures you want to do but can’t right now. You can add your side quests, view them all, get a random one when you’re free, and keep a visual history of the ones you’ve completed.

Built as a fun personal productivity + motivation tool, SideQuest turns your bucket list ideas into something actionable and exciting. All content is created by you — making every list completely personal.

Feel free to start adding your own side quests today!
