About the
Side Quest List

Hi, I'm K — and I built this because I kept saying "I'd love to do that someday" without ever actually doing it.

The Side Quest List is your personal collection of all the things you want to do but haven't had time for yet. Add your ideas, browse them when you're free, or let the site pick one for you. When you finish a quest, log it — with photos, reflections, whatever you like.

Life's too short for a to-do list. This is your adventure list.
